## MVP

Minimum Viable Platform: a small, non-production set of paths (three, in the book's 3x3 method) built for one team by about three people within about three months. Its job is to prove demand and teach the team, not to be the final architecture; it may be thrown away. Identity, central policy and state are deliberately left out until Production Readiness. *Earned in Lessons 2 and 3.*
